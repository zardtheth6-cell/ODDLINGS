# AFTERHOURS Launch and Store Page Plan

## Positioning
AFTERHOURS is a tense, atmospheric horror exploration and extraction experience. Players enter dangerous locations, investigate, collect what they need and try to make it out. Players can experience the danger alone, with friends or from the Creature role.

Do not position it as a shooter. Do not promise features that are not implemented and verified.

## Audience promise
- Believable, atmospheric locations.
- Stealth and movement that matter.
- Unpredictable environmental events.
- Distinct Creature behavior and cinematic captures.
- Solo and multiplayer play.
- Meaningful progression and cosmetic identity.

## Store page requirements
- Short, clear title and description.
- Original icon readable at small sizes.
- Thumbnail showing a distinctive AFTERHOURS moment, not a cluttered collage.
- Screenshots from actual in-game builds, not concept art presented as gameplay.
- Accurate mode and player-count information.
- Honest feature list and age-appropriate presentation.

## Trailer structure
1. Quiet establishing shot of the Mall after closing.
2. Explorer enters and begins an objective.
3. Environmental event changes the space.
4. Brief Creature glimpse.
5. Movement and stealth moment.
6. Cooperative objective or rescue moment.
7. Cinematic capture beat.
8. Extraction tension.
9. AFTERHOURS title and concise call to play.

Only capture footage from the actual build. Avoid implying features that do not exist.

## Launch content checklist
- Experience icon.
- At least two clear thumbnails.
- Short and long descriptions.
- Accurate screenshots.
- Trailer or short gameplay clip if ready.
- Moderated support/community route if available.
- Update notes and known-issues process.

## Marketing experiments
Test thumbnail readability, title clarity and first-session conversion. Use honest creative variations. Do not use misleading promises, fake player counts or artificial urgency.

## Release communication
Explain what is new, what has changed, what is experimental and where players can report issues. Never announce a date until the release gate is passed.
