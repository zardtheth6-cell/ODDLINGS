## Every room must have a room card
For each room ID, Claude must record:
- Approximate dimensions and ceiling height.
- Floor, wall and ceiling construction.
- Door/window types and opening direction.
- Main prop groups and unique landmark.
- Practical light sources and emergency light.
- Ambient sound identity.
- Public / staff / restricted access classification.
- Explorer route role and Creature route role.
- Hiding spots and explicit rules, if any.
- Objective/loot spawn nodes, if any.
- Streaming and performance notes.
- Screenshot reference after implementation.

## Dressing density
Do not fill every square stud. Keep primary paths clear. Cluster props around believable functions: counters have work equipment; staff rooms have staff items; loading areas have freight; maintenance rooms have plant equipment. Use negative space to make the location feel large and unsettling. Repetition must be broken through layout, prop variation and lighting—not random clutter.

## Quality control
Review every room at player eye height, from the doorway, from its main route and from the Creature's approach. Remove floating props, intersecting meshes, impossible doors, duplicated clutter clusters, text too small to read and decorative collision that catches players.
