## Required reference collection
### Environments
Mall exterior/entrance, central atrium, upper floor, escalators, food court, department store, retail corridors, service corridors, loading dock, security room, maintenance plant, parking garage, hotel lobby, guest corridors, guest rooms, laundry, kitchen, service elevator and headquarters.

### Maps and layouts
Mall floor 1 and floor 2 top-down layouts; Mall service route; Hotel floor plan; Explorer routes; Creature traversal routes; hiding spaces; safe spawn zones; objective/loot zones; extraction zones. All maps must be original blockout diagrams with dimensions, labels, gameplay notes and sightline notes.

### Creatures
Front/side/back turnarounds; scale comparison beside Roblox avatar; face close-ups; material sheets; silhouette at multiple distances; idle, patrol, stalk, sprint, crawl/climb, attack, capture, recovery and victory poses; Explorer encounter view; Creature-player POV.

### UI
Main menu, headquarters HUD, expedition selection, party lobby, briefing, Explorer HUD, inventory, equipment shop, item detail, settings, profile, progression, contract board, pause, defeat/capture, spectate, Creature selection, Creature HUD, results and loading screens. Include PC, mobile and controller variants, plus hover/focus, pressed, disabled, loading, empty, error and confirmation states.

## Art direction
Photorealistic-feeling architectural visualization; cinematic horror; realistic scale; restrained charcoal/black/aged neutral palette; subtle warm practical lights; emergency red only when contextually justified; nuanced reflections; believable material roughness; environmental storytelling; natural signage; minimal UI; premium legible typography.

## Strict exclusions
No guns, firearms, ammunition, crosshairs, tactical military equipment, soldier styling, shooter HUD, cartoon buttons, toy-like monsters, low-detail graybox presented as final, copied IP creatures, copied commercial UI, illegible decorative text or fake controls.

## Asset provenance
For every external reference record source URL, creator/rights status, date accessed and intended use. Prefer original generated art or properly licensed material. Never import an image/model into Roblox unless usage rights are clear.
