## Asset rules
- Keep concept art separate from production-ready Roblox assets.
- Do not treat reference images as permission to copy third-party intellectual property.
- Record source, creator, license and date for external assets.
- Prefer original models, textures, UI icons, audio and animations.
- Validate asset dimensions, texture memory, collision, rigging and platform performance.
- Never publish placeholder, unlicensed or unreviewed assets as final content.

## Required asset groups
Environment kits; Mall architecture; Hotel architecture; props and clutter; materials and textures; signs and decals; Explorer animations; Creature rigs and animations; UI icons and panels; audio; VFX; thumbnails and marketing images.
