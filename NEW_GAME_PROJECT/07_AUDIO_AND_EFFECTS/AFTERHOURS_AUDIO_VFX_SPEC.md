# AFTERHOURS — Audio and VFX Direction

## Audio identity
Sound is a primary gameplay information channel. Use believable spatial audio and dynamic mix rather than constant horror music. Quiet is intentional.

### Environmental layers
HVAC hum, electrical buzz, ventilation, refrigeration, fluorescent ballast, distant elevator motors, water drip, building creaks, traffic, wind through entrances and room-specific ambience. Footsteps differ on tile, carpet, concrete, metal grating, wet floor and debris. Include door, drawer, locker, elevator, shutter and container sounds; PA system, radio/static, alarm and emergency power.

### Human sounds
Breathing changes with sprint/stamina. Footsteps match movement state and surface. Jump landing and sliding create distinct noise. Avoid loud repetitive breathing at rest. Crouching is quieter but not perfectly silent.

### Creature sounds
Each Creature has unique idle, movement, detection, ability, chase, attack, capture and recovery sounds. Give clear but not overly obvious tells. Creature player hears role-specific mix without unfairly hearing hidden Explorers.

### Jumpscare mix
Each capture has authored sound timing: pre-attack cue, movement/impact transient, creature vocalization, brief tonal impact and transition. Avoid extreme volume spikes. Respect master/SFX sliders and accessibility settings. Do not use copyrighted music clips.

## Visual effects
Subtle dust, haze and light shafts where they improve depth. Practical light flicker tied to fixtures/events. Emergency lighting and localized power failure. Flashlight beam with controlled brightness/falloff and battery state. Restrained particles for dust, water and debris. Capture effects are brief and configurable. Avoid heavy full-screen blur, chromatic aberration and excessive camera shake. Profile on low-end devices.

## Lighting
Use Roblox lighting technology appropriate to device capability. Build consistent ambient lighting; use dynamic lights only where necessary. Use reflection/roughness response selectively. Keep exits and interaction targets legible. Do not hide the whole map in pure blackness.

## Technical checks
Test audio attenuation, emitter placement, looping, cleanup and replication. Pool VFX where practical and clean them up. Provide graphics quality options and reduced visual intensity.
