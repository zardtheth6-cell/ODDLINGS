# AFTERHOURS — Project Brief

Status: approved concept / pre-production.

AFTERHOURS is an ultra-realistic-feeling Roblox horror exploration, stealth, survival and extraction game. Players enter closed buildings as ordinary Explorers with flashlights, backpacks, keys, batteries and practical exploration tools. They investigate, complete objectives, collect valuables, avoid danger and escape. A separate asymmetrical mode lets a player control a Creature and hunt Explorers.

NON-NEGOTIABLE: no guns, firearms, shooting, ammunition, weapon loadouts, crosshairs, military styling or shooter HUD. This is not a shooting game.

Confirmed pillars: extreme environmental realism; hybrid psychological and creature horror; unique cinematic capture jumpscares; walk, sprint, crouch, jump, slide, vault, lean and hide; realistic minimal UI; playable Creature role; complete solo and multiplayer; no voice chat dependency; replayable objectives, loot, events and secrets.

Initial target: 1–4 Explorers, solo vs AI, one player-controlled Creature vs 1–4 Explorers, The Mall as flagship map, The Hotel only after Mall quality approval, three initial Creature archetypes (The Watcher, The Crawler, The Employee).

Build one polished vertical slice before expanding content. Keep planned, implemented and tested statuses separate. Do not reuse ODDLINGS assets or scripts. Create original assets and record provenance/licensing for external references.
